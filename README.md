Spain is a vibrant southwestern European destination celebrated for its rich artistic heritage, diverse regional cultures, high-speed rail networks, and Mediterranean and Atlantic coastlines. This list is intended for cultural travelers, architectural explorers, culinary tourists, and remote professionals.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, Schengen visa info, and weather tracking.

- [Spain.info (Official Tourism Portal - Turespaña)](https://www.spain.info/en/) - Official national tourist board portal featuring destination guides, cultural itineraries, and regional highlights.
- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visas](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/Services/ServicesForForeigners/Pages/Visas.aspx) - Official consular portal for Schengen visa entry regulations, passport requirements, and short-stay rules.
- [AEMET Weather (State Meteorological Agency)](https://www.aemet.es/en/portada) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, severe storm warnings, and climate radar data.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating high-speed rail networks, intercity trains, long-distance coaches, and domestic aviation.

- [Renfe (National Railway Operator)](https://www.renfe.com/es/en) - National railway operator managing AVE high-speed bullet trains and regional rail networks across Spain.
- [Ouigo Spain & Iryo](https://www.ouigo.com/es/en) - Private high-speed rail operators offering frequent passenger connections between Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and Andalusia.
- [ALSA Bus Lines](https://www.alsa.com/en) - Major long-distance coach network connecting regional towns, coastal destinations, and international routes.
- [Iberia](https://www.iberia.com/us/) - National flag carrier operating international long-haul flights and domestic connections across the Iberian Peninsula and Balearic/Canary Islands.

## Destinations & Regions

### Madrid & Central Spain

- [Turismo de Madrid (Official City Portal)](https://www.esmadrid.com/en) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Prado Museum, Royal Palace, historic neighborhoods, and regional day trips (Toledo, Segovia).
- [Renfe Cercanías Madrid](https://www.renfe.com/) - Official commuter rail and transit guide for navigating the capital's metropolitan area.

### Barcelona & Catalonia

- [Barcelona Turisme (Official City Portal)](https://www.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring Antoni Gaudí's architectural works, the Gothic Quarter, and Mediterranean beaches.
- [Catalan Tourist Board (Catalunya.com)](https://www.catalunya.com/) - Regional portal for discovering Costa Brava, Pyrenees mountain villages, and local vineyards.

### Andalusia & Southern Spain

- [Andalusia Tourism Portal (Andalucía.org)](https://www.andalucia.org/en) - Official regional visitor hub for exploring the Alhambra in Granada, the Mezquita in Córdoba, Seville's Alcázar, and Costa del Sol beaches.
- [Alhambra Official Ticket Portal](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/) - Official reservation and ticketing site for visiting Granada's iconic Moorish palace complex.

### Basque Country & Northern Coast

- [Euskadi Tourism (Basque Country)](https://tourism.euskadi.eus/en/) - Official regional portal for San Sebastián's pintxo culinary culture, Bilbao's Guggenheim Museum, and coastal fishing ports.
- [Turismo de Asturias](https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/) - Official travel guide for Picos de Europa National Park, green valleys, and cider houses.

### Valencia & Eastern Coast

- [Visit Valencia (Official City Portal)](https://www.visitvalencia.com/en) - Official municipal visitor guide for the City of Arts and Sciences, historic silk exchange, and coastal beaches.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Spain's Digital Nomad Visa framework.

- [Spain Digital Nomad Visa (UGE-CE / Moncloa)](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/) - Official government portal detailing residency permits, income requirements, and tax incentives for non-EU remote workers.
- [Aticco Coworking Barcelona](https://aticco.com/) - Popular network of flexible workspaces, innovation hubs, and community networking spaces across Spanish cities.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and local safety rules.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.interior.gob.es/) - Integrated single European emergency hotline for police (Policía Nacional / Guardia Civil), fire brigade, and medical ambulance assistance.
